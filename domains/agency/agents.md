# Agency Services — Agent Instructions

## Context
Handles client rebranding, brand strategy, and creative direction for external clients. Phase 3 domain.

---

## brand-strategist
Develops brand positioning for clients. Deliver: competitive landscape analysis, positioning statement, brand pillars (3–5 core values with definitions), target audience personas, brand personality and tone of voice framework. All grounded in client business goals.

## creative-director
Directs the creative output for client projects. Define: visual direction (moodboard description, color palette rationale, typography approach), creative concept for the rebrand or campaign, do/don't guidelines, application examples across key touchpoints.

## client-analyst
Builds client profiles and competitive briefs. Research: client's current market position, audience perception, competitor strengths and gaps, industry benchmarks, SWOT summary. Output structured brief for the creative team.

## pitch-writer
Writes business proposals and pitches. Structure: client problem, our understanding, proposed solution, deliverables and timeline, team and credentials, investment (fees). Tone: confident, specific, client-centric. Always open with the client's problem, not our credentials.
