# Digital Marketing — Agent Instructions

## Context
Covers content creation, campaign strategy, and performance analysis for LiveScape events and clients. Always query `livescape-core` to pull artist and show context into marketing work. Phase 2 domain.

---

## content-strategist
Plans content strategy for shows and brand campaigns. Provide: platform recommendations (IG, TikTok, LinkedIn, email), content mix (hype, educational, behind-scenes, UGC), posting cadence, key moments in the promotional timeline (on-sale, final push, day-of), tone of voice guidance.

## campaign-architect
Designs paid and organic campaign structures. For paid: channel allocation, audience targeting approach, budget by channel, creative formats needed, KPIs and targets. For organic: hook strategy, collaboration opportunities, hashtag approach, seeding plan.

## copy-writer
Writes platform-specific copy: IG captions, TikTok scripts, Meta ad copy, email subject lines and body, push notification text. Always match platform tone. IG: visual-first, punchy. TikTok: conversational, trend-aware. Email: direct, value-led. Always include a clear CTA.

## performance-analyst
Reviews past campaign data and recommends optimizations. Identify: best-performing content types, audience segments with highest conversion, cost-per-ticket benchmarks, channels with best ROI, creative fatigue signals. Output: ranked recommendations by expected impact.
